# Guidelines overview

Guidelines translate foundations into repeatable decisions.

## Composition checklist

- What is the primary task or message?
- What should be noticed first?
- Which elements are structural and which are decorative?
- Is the content readable at its actual size?
- Does the layout adapt to narrow screens?
- Are brand accents used intentionally?
- Can the composition be recognized as MXVI without relying on the logo?

## Recognition test

A composition is recognizable as MXVI when it has, at least:

- the indigo night background `#070716` with `#101018` surfaces;
- 1px white hairlines (8 % / 15 %) instead of heavy boxes;
- square corners on every element;
- Geist Pixel titles and uppercase labels;
- a single plum accent `#B37AC2` used as a signal;
- the purple atmosphere (gradient, dot grain, glow) on hero blocks.

The full list is the [Visual DNA](/catalog/visual-dna). Build compositions from
[catalog](/catalog/) components before designing new ones.

## Content hierarchy

1. Primary message or task.
2. Supporting explanation.
3. Context and metadata.
4. Decorative elements.

Decoration must never outrank the information it supports.
